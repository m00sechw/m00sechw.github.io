RowMinder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Developer: 8292116 Canada Inc.

RowMinder is a row counter for knitting and crochet. It is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains, plainly, what that means.

What RowMinder collects

Nothing. RowMinder collects no personal data, has no accounts or sign-up, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Your data stays on your phone

Your projects, counts, history, settings, and any photos you add are stored only on your device. RowMinder makes no network connections and sends no data to us or to any third party. The app works fully offline.

Photos

If you add photos of your work, RowMinder stores a resized copy inside the app on your device. Photos are never uploaded anywhere by the app. They leave your device only if you choose to share them yourself (for example, with the share card) or include them in a backup file you export.

Backups

The backup feature creates a single file (your data plus photo copies) that you save or share wherever you choose. Where that file goes is entirely up to you; we never receive it.

Purchases

The optional RowMinder Unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

RowMinder is a general-audience craft tool. It does not collect data from anyone, including children.
