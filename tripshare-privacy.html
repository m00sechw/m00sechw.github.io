TripShare: Vacation Countdown — Privacy Policy

Published by 8292116 Canada Inc. · Effective October 19, 2026

TripShare is designed to be private by design. The app works entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. TripShare does not collect, transmit, store on servers, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically unable to send your data anywhere.

Information stored on your device

Trips you create (destinations, travel dates and times, travel details, and packing lists) are stored only in the app's private storage on your own device. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (see below). You can delete it at any time by removing the trips in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Sharing features

If you choose to share a countdown image or send a trip file to a companion, the app prepares that content and hands it to the app you select (for example, a messaging or email app). That sharing is initiated and controlled entirely by you, and the receiving app's own privacy policy applies to anything you send through it. Trip files never include booking or confirmation numbers or payment details.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information; Google's handling of purchase data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Children

TripShare is a general-audience travel app and is not directed at children. As stated above, it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
