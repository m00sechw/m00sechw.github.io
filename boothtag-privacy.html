Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026
Developer: 8292116 Canada Inc., Whitby, Ontario, Canada
Contact: Paul.dev.murray@gmail.com

The short version: BoothTag is designed to work on your device. The products, brand information, and settings you enter stay in the app’s storage on your device. BoothTag has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated server for collecting your information. Google Play processes the optional one-time unlock purchase.

Information stored by the app

When you use BoothTag, the app stores the information you choose to enter or select so that it can create your signs, including:

  • product names, prices, categories, notes, badges, and product lists imported from a CSV file;
  • your brand kit, such as business name, tagline, logo image, colours, font and text-size choices;
  • sign content and settings, including payment-method labels, QR code links or images, social-media handles or links, testimonials, offers, and other text you provide for particular signs;
  • app preferences such as language, currency, paper size, selected templates and formats, and whether the BoothTag unlock is active on the device.

This information is stored locally in BoothTag’s app storage and, where applicable, in files on your device. We do not receive a copy merely because you use the app.

No internet permission for BoothTag’s own functions

BoothTag's own features never use the internet and the developer operates no backend server for the app. One technical exception: the app package includes the Android INTERNET permission because Google Play Billing requires it to process the optional unlock purchase and purchase restores through Google Play; BoothTag itself makes no network calls. The app does not transmit your products, prices, brand kit, settings, or generated signs to 8292116 Canada Inc.

BoothTag does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting software development kits (SDKs) for collecting information about you. Local libraries used for features such as QR-code generation and fonts run on the device.

How local information is used

The app uses the information stored on your device only to provide the features you request: saving your products and brand kit, formatting prices and signs, generating QR codes, previewing designs, and creating PDF or PNG files. We do not sell your information, and BoothTag does not share it with third parties.

If you export a sign and then send, upload, print, or share that file using another app or service, you control that disclosure and the other service’s privacy policy applies.

Purchases through Google Play

The optional BoothTag unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes the payment information needed to complete the transaction under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. 8292116 Canada Inc. does not receive your payment-card number or other payment credentials. BoothTag receives purchase-entitlement information from Google Play so the app can determine whether the unlock is owned and activate the paid features.

Signing in to Google Play, completing a purchase, and restoring a purchase use Google Play and may require an internet connection. Creating and exporting signs in BoothTag works offline.

QR codes and external links

You may enter a website, shop, payment, review, social-media, or other link for a QR code. BoothTag converts or displays that information locally. BoothTag does not operate the linked website or service and does not track who scans a printed QR code. When a person follows a link or scans a code, the destination service may process information under its own privacy policy.

Android backups and exported files

Android’s optional device-backup features, if enabled in your system settings, are provided by Android and Google and may include app data in a device backup. Those system features are controlled by your Android and Google settings, not by BoothTag. PDF and PNG files that you export are stored where you choose and remain under your control; deleting the app does not automatically delete files you previously exported or shared.

Your choices and control

  • You can edit or delete products and brand information inside the app where those editing controls are provided.
  • You can remove BoothTag’s local app data by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings.
  • Uninstalling BoothTag removes the app and its local app storage from the device, subject to any separate Android device backup you control.
  • You can delete exported PDF and PNG files using your file manager or the app/service where you saved them.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use that information to read and respond to your request and to provide support. Please do not send payment-card information by email.

Security

BoothTag keeps its working data in Android app storage on your device. The security of that information also depends on your device, screen lock, Android settings, backups, and the people or services with whom you share exported files. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

BoothTag is a business and productivity tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information, contact us at the email address above and we will address the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if BoothTag’s features or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted at this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s Google Play listing or in the app where appropriate.
