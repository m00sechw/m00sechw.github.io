Privacy Policy — Blood in the Snow (BITS Festival) App

Effective date: October 19, 2026
Developer: 8292116 Canada Inc.
Contact: paul.murrayp@gmail.com

The Blood in the Snow (BITS Festival) app is a schedule and wayfinding guide for the Blood in the Snow Film Festival in Toronto. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app collects no personal data at all.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The developer receives no information about you or your device from the app.

Information stored on your device

Events you save and your reminder settings are stored only on your own device, so the app can show your personal schedule and fire your reminders. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Notifications

If you turn on event reminders, the app asks Android for the notifications permission. Reminders are generated entirely on your device. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app's settings, or revoke the permission in Android settings.

Links to other services

The app contains links that open in your browser — for example the festival website, venue directions, and film pages on IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, and Letterboxd. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave the app.

Children

The festival features horror films intended for adult audiences, and this app is intended for users 18 and older.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
