FreezerStash — Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

FreezerStash is a freezer, fridge and pantry inventory app published by 8292116 Canada Inc. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: your list stays on your device, and we collect none of it.

Data we collect

None. FreezerStash has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The items, places, photos and settings you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your own device. They are never transmitted to us.

Barcode lookup

When you scan a barcode or tap "Look up", the app sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), the free community food database, to fetch that product's name. No personal information, no device identifiers, and none of your inventory are sent. If Open Food Facts has no match, the app asks you to name the product once, and remembers your name for that barcode on your device.

Camera, photos and microphone

The camera is used to scan barcodes and to take item photos; scanning and photo analysis happen on your device, and photos are stored only in the app's private storage. Voice entry is optional and uses your device's built-in speech recognition. The weekly use-first digest notification is generated entirely on your device.

Backups, exports and sharing

The app lets you export your data (CSV and backup files) and share your stash with family members. Those files are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them, using your device's standard share options. We never receive a copy.

Purchases

The optional one-time Unlock is sold through Google Play. Your payment is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy; we do not see or store your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation from Google Play that the Unlock is owned, so it can enable the unlocked features.

Children

FreezerStash is a household tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
