MapleMargin — Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

MapleMargin is a maker pricing calculator published by 8292116 Canada Inc. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: your data stays on your device, and we collect none of it.

Data we collect

None. MapleMargin has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The products, materials, prices and settings you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your own device. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Backups and exports

The app lets you export your data (CSV, PDF, and backup files). Those files are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them, using your device's standard share options. We never receive a copy.

Purchases

The optional one-time Unlock is sold through Google Play. Your payment is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy; we do not see or store your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation from Google Play that the Unlock is owned, so it can enable the unlocked features.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files outside its own storage, other than files you explicitly choose to import or export.

Children

MapleMargin is a business tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
